# Treatment: swiss-programme

Style: Swiss Motion Graphics (swiss-motion, styles/swiss-motion). Read its STYLE.md first; DEMO.md and the demo source only after this treatment exists.

## Brief

Topic: what a style pack is, told in the style itself: a style is a programme, a set of rules, plus one exception.
Audience: people opening the Moodreel repo. Length 15 s, 1920×1080, 24 fps, silent (the beat grid is 120 BPM so a
track can be laid under it later). Words that must appear: "rule 1 — measure first", "rule 2 — one signal colour",
"measure", "programme".

## Three structures

1. A poster assembled by rules, then many posters from the same rules (the demo's structure: rejected).
2. Two rules, each with its own page; a page turn between them; the exception arrives from outside the system;
   a knife cuts the system away and leaves the exception and one word.
3. One numeral counting up from 1 to 5 while the grid tightens (too long for 15 s).

Chosen: 2, because it fits two chapters into 15 s and ends on the idea: rules are what you cut away, the exception
and the name are what stay.

## Logline and arc

A dot becomes a grid, the grid becomes two rules, and a red square that obeys neither is all that is left.
Setup: dot, grid, rule 1 with its bars and the word "measure". Turn: a page turn to rule 2; the red square slides in
linearly and stops off the grid, with its coordinate. Ending: a knife crosses "2", the system is cut away nearest
first, and "programme" rises in the corner.

## Shots

| # | time | framing | camera | duration | why |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0–5.5 s | spread 1 | locked | 5.5 s | build the system where it can be read |
| 2 | 5.5–6 s | page turn | pans one half-frame (−960 px), precise ease, lands on beat 12 | 0.5 s | the only camera move |
| 3 | 6–15 s | spread 2 | locked | 9 s | rule 2, the exception, the cut, the end word |

## Beats

120 BPM, beat = 0.5 s. Snaps start one note early and land on the beat.

- 0.5 s (b1): the dot lands; grid lines grow linearly from the centre line, one column every sixteenth.
- 2 s (b4): "1" rises out of its clip; the dot is taken back. 2.5 s (b5): rule and caption 1.
- 3–5.5 s: six bars rise on eighths from b6. 4.5 s (b9): "measure" rises.
- 6 s (b12): page turn lands. 6.5 s (b13): "2" rises; 7 s (b14) on: eight squares on sixteenths; 7.5 s (b15): caption 2.
- 8.5–10 s (b17–b20): the red square slides in linearly from off frame, stops off the grid; its readout appears at 10 s.
- 10.5 s (b21): the knife crosses "2" (linear, an eighth); 11.25 s (b22.5) "2" collapses to its midline.
- 11.75–12.75 s: everything else collapses, sixteenth by sixteenth, nearest to the knife first.
- 12.75 s (b25.5): "programme" rises in the corner; hold to 15 s.

## Not the demo

Structure: two pages, not one poster built by five rules. Opening: a dot, not a red strike. Signature shot: the
exception entering from outside the frame and the system cut away around it, not a 90° rotation into a score.
Ending: one word and the exception, not a wall of posters.
