# Treatment: first-light

Style: Risograph Print (risograph, styles/risograph). Read its STYLE.md first; DEMO.md and the demo source only after this treatment exists.

## Brief

A 12 s title piece with no words but its title: "first light". 1920×1080, 24 fps, silent. It exists to show the
style's materials (two plates, overprint, halftone tints, misregistration, grain, drawings on twos) in a film short
enough to distil back.

## Three structures

1. Plates as narrative: the world gains a plate at each turning point (the demo's structure: rejected).
2. Two plates, one locked composition: the sea is printed, the sun rises on the second plate, a swimmer crosses,
   the title is printed on both, then the second plate lifts away (one ink left).
3. Dots first: a push out of a halftone field into the picture (needs a camera move; not for 12 s).

Chosen: 2, because one locked frame lets each print event read on its own, and the ending (one ink left) is one of
the style's suggested endings that the demo does not use.

## Logline and arc

A sea in one ink, a sun in another; where they meet is a third colour, and when the sun's plate lifts, the morning
is printed. Setup: paper, the sea. Turn: the sun rises, its reflection overprints the sea. Ending: the title in
both plates, a registration kick, the orange plate lifts away.

## Beats

Drawings, sweeps and the kick on twos (12 drawings a second); the camera is locked.

- 0–0.6 s: paper.
- 0.6–1.4 s: the teal plate's sea arrives under the roller, left to right (linear).
- 1.5–3.5 s: the sun rises from behind the horizon (power2.out, 2 s); its reflection bars widen with it and overprint the sea.
- 3.5 s: two stepped teal tints (40 %, 20 %) print the sky on a cut.
- 4–8 s: a swimmer crosses the sea at constant speed, one stroke a second, with a paper knockout halo.
- 8.5–9 s: the title is printed on both plates under the roller (a double-hit, 4 px out of register).
- 9.5 s: the orange plate kicks (12 px, gone in a quarter second).
- 10.5–11 s: the orange plate lifts away left to right; the title, the sea and the sky stay in teal. Hold to 12 s.

## Not the demo

Structure: one locked shot, not a ride through three places. Opening: a sea under the roller, not a plate per stop.
Signature shot: the sun's reflection overprinting the sea. Ending: one ink left, not three plates overprinting.
