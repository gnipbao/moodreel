# Treatment: boot

Style: ASCII CRT (ascii-crt, styles/ascii-crt). Read its STYLE.md first; DEMO.md and the demo source only after this treatment exists.

## Brief

A 10 s title for Moodreel on an amber terminal: the machine wakes, reports itself, types its name, leans in, and
switches off. 1920×1080, 24 fps, silent. Words: the boot log (picture, not subtitles), MOODREEL, > SYSTEM READY.

## Three structures

1. A chat log across a night (needs a voice and many lines).
2. A boot-to-shutdown day compressed into one boot: power on, log, clear, title, push in, power off.
3. A crash dump rewound (an error first; needs the scroll buffer).

Chosen: 2, because the style's own camera (the grid scale) and its cuts (clear screen, power) carry it.

## Beats

- 0.3–0.55 s: power-on line grows from the centre; 0.55–0.8 s the raster opens and its flash decays (τ 0.12 s).
- 0.9–3.0 s: the boot log, typed at 10 ms a character, OK in bold; the cursor blinks until 4.0 s.
- 4.0 s: clear screen (a cut); MOODREEL typed with key clicks (50 ms a character); the LOG strip types its words.
- 4.6–6.2 s: push in: the grid's cells grow five times about the title (power2.inOut); hold.
- 8.5 s: power off: squash to a line (0.15 s), the line to a dot (0.15 s), the dot glows out.

## Not the demo

Structure: one machine's boot, not a story told in pictures. Opening: power on. Signature: the push into the
title. Ending: power off to a dot.
