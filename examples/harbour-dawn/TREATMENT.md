# Treatment: harbour-dawn

Style: Watercolor Brush (watercolor, styles/watercolor). Read its STYLE.md first; DEMO.md and the demo source only after this treatment exists.

## Brief

A 10 s establishing shot: a harbour paints itself in at dawn, one buoy is labelled, a quiet title. 1920×1080, 24 fps,
silent. Words: buoy · 06:12, Harbour, dawn. It exists to show painted marks in Moodreel: kit/brush.js washes,
bristled strokes and ink, laid in slices of time, structure first.

## Three structures

1. A field guide page: a plant painting itself in with labelled parts (the style's natural home; needs more words).
2. A harbour at dawn painting in, layer by layer, one annotated detail, a title.
3. A weather map whose palette carries rainfall (colour as data; needs a data set).

Chosen: 2, the plainest "things paint themselves in, stroke by stroke", with the annotation layer at the end.

## Beats

- 0.0–1.2 s: the sky washes in behind a wavy brush edge; 0.3–1.4 s five broad strokes cross it, bristles breaking
  into dashes; 0.8–2.2 s the water; 1.6–2.6 s a far shore, one wash.
- 1.8–3.0 s: ripples, horizontal strokes painted along their length.
- 2.4–3.6 s: three hulls washed in, staggered 0.3 s; 3.6–4.8 s their masts drawn up in ink; 4.6–5.6 s reflections,
  short dabs under each hull, fading downward. From 5 s the masts sway (a skew of about 0.006).
- 5.4–6.0 s: the accent, reserved for one thing: two vermilion buoys.
- 6.2 s: a pen leader line rises from a buoy; 6.3 s its label opens left to right; 6.8 s the title; hold to 10 s.

## Not the demo

Structure: one place at one hour, not a sequence of plants. Opening: a brush-edge wipe of the sky. Signature: hulls
and masts painting in with their reflections. Ending: a labelled buoy and a quiet title.
