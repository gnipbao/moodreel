# Treatment: morning

Style: Pictogram Motion (pictogram-motion, styles/pictogram-motion). Read its STYLE.md first; DEMO.md and the demo source only after this treatment exists.

## Brief

A 12 s catalogue of three morning actions, one card each, one chapter in one hue (a coastal teal). 1920×1080,
24 fps, silent, 120 BPM, a card per two bars (4 s), cut on the downbeat. Words: WAKE / 起床, BREW / 煮咖啡,
WALK / 散步, M O R N I N G, 01 / 03.

## Three structures

1. A countdown scrolling down rows (needs a vertical camera; long).
2. A day in pictograms, one card per action, palette held in one hue for the chapter.
3. An assembly of one growing machine (needs many parts).

Chosen: 2, three cards, so each card's choreography repeats and can be measured three times.

## Beats (per card, from its downbeat)

- 0.1–0.5 s: the sun disc pops from 60 % (expo.out), landing on beat 1.
- 0.25–1.0 s: the figure slides into register in three bands from alternating sides, a sixteenth apart (expo.out).
- 1.0–1.5 s: the title rises out of its slot (mask slide-up, expo.out), landing on beat 3; the Chinese line 0.08 s later.
- 2.5–3.5 s: the action: the figure lifts on beat 6 and settles on beat 7 (sine.inOut).
- 4.0 s: cut to the next card, its own pattern.

## Not the demo

Structure: three cards in one hue, not a multi-chapter catalogue. Opening: the sun first. Signature: the figure's
bands. Ending: the third card held, no outro.
