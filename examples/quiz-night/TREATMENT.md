# Treatment: quiz-night

Style: Game Show Flat (game-show, styles/game-show). Read its STYLE.md first; DEMO.md and the demo source only after this treatment exists.

## Brief

A 12 s game show about telling film styles apart: three contestants, two rounds, a winner. 1920×1080, 24 fps, its own
score at 120 BPM (`score.json`), no narration. Words: WHICH STYLE?, SWISS!, RIGHT!, ROUND 2, WINNER!, and the
contestants' names. It exists to show a music-led film: the grid and the cues first, the picture landing on them.

## Three structures

1. A leaderboard climb: one contestant per bar, the board scrolling up (needs a longer film).
2. Two rounds: a question with a held breath and a stamp, then a hard cut on the bar line to the scoreboard and a winner.
3. Human vs machine on a split stage (the style's split, but two halves halve the cast).

Chosen: 2, the style's "a round = a repeatable verb + a win", in the smallest form that still has a cut on a bar line.

## Beats

120 BPM, beat 0.5 s, 6 bars; every event is a cue in score.json and a sound.

- Bar 1: tangerine stripes; the contestants drop from the sky, one per beat, landing on beats 2, 3 and 4 (pops).
- Bar 2: the round card pops on the downbeat (a stab); beat 3 the band stops for a beat and the cast freezes under a
  "?"; beat 4 SWISS answers (a ding).
- Bar 3: RIGHT! stamps with a burst on the downbeat (a hit, a punch of the frame, a squash of the winner).
- Bar 4: a hard cut on the bar line with a two-frame white flash to teal dots; ROUND 2 sticker; beat 3 the
  scoreboard pops (a stab).
- Bar 5: SWISS's score bumps to 2 (a boing). Bar 6: a riser ends on the downbeat; WINNER! (a hit) and a shout on
  every other beat to the end.

## Not the demo

Structure: two quiz rounds, not a history in rounds. Opening: three drops on beats 2-4. Signature: the band stopping
for one beat while the cast freezes. Ending: a scoreboard and a shout, not a report card.
