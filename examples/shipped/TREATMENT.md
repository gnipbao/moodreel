# Treatment: shipped

Style: Dark Keynote (dark-keynote, styles/dark-keynote). Read its STYLE.md first; DEMO.md and the demo source only after this treatment exists.

## Brief

A 10 s release beat for a software version: a checklist completes, the version number takes the screen, one lit
tile and a toast say it shipped. 1920×1080, 24 fps, silent, beat grid 120 BPM. Words: build, tests, docs, deploy,
2.0, shipped.

## Three structures

1. A zoom ladder from one pixel to a fleet (needs a 2.5D camera; long).
2. A countdown: a release checklist ticks on the beat, the window switches off, the number alone, one tile left.
3. Side by side, old and new pipeline (needs two halves running at once; too dense for 10 s).

Chosen: 2, the style's suggested "countdown" structure with its "one tile left" ending, not the demo's.

## Beats

120 BPM, beat 0.5 s; spawns land on beats with back.out(1.3).

- 0.7–1.0 s: the window expands from its centre (0.3 s).
- 0.75–2.5 s: four rows slide in from 60 px right, one per beat; each check pops on the next beat.
- 3.5 s: the window switches off like a tube: squash to a line (0.15 s, power2.in), then to a dot (0.1 s).
- 4.2–4.5 s: "2.0" pops from 60 %; holds alone with no caption; 6.5 s it squashes off.
- 6.75–7.0 s: one lime tile pops at the centre; 7.5 s the toast rises and fades in (0.16 s); hold to 10 s.

## Not the demo

Structure: a checklist countdown. Opening: an empty stage, then a window expanding. Signature: the CRT-off squash
of a whole window. Ending: one tile and a toast.
